About

My work focuses on helping people make decisions when outcomes are uncertain. I independently build analytical applications that collect, validate, model, and present complex sports data in a form people can use.

Before moving into analytics, I spent nineteen years as an elementary teacher. That experience strengthened the skills that still shape my work today: explaining complex ideas clearly, designing structured processes, evaluating evidence, and improving decisions through continuous feedback.

Sports is where I have demonstrated these capabilities, but the underlying process is broader: define the problem, test assumptions, validate results, communicate limitations, and build something useful.
